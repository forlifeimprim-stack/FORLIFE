## The seven questions
1. **Say / tone:** see the sentence above. Pain is urgent and small (one person, late at night); relief is warm; proof is trustworthy (a human taps Valider).
2. **Repeats:** six transitions, five families (cut, mask, carry, time, camera), each used once except the deliberate `motif:cut` base.
3. **Meaning of each transition:**
   - 1→2 hard cut: the pile keeps growing, same night.
   - 2→3 beat-cut-hold: "wait for it", and the drop sweeps the pile away.
   - 3→4 product-shape mask: the Forlife F opens onto the team; the brand *is* the team.
   - 4→5 object carry: the copywriter badge walks into the prompt; the team gets to work.
   - 5→6 time-lapse tick: results arrive later, not instantly.
   - 6→7 dolly-out: from one phone to the whole calm counter.
4. **Colour over time:** night-paper and wood until the drop; orange floods once at the reveal; neutral paper during the proof (the content is the colour); WhatsApp green appears once, in the notifications; orange returns for the CTA.
5. **New component:** team-badges-lanyard. Staff ID badges on lanyards swing like pendulums (damped sine) and then reel into the phone.
6. **Surprises:**
   - ≈5.5 s: freeze + silence.
   - ≈17.5 s: freeze before the tap.
   - 24 s: scale jump on the pull-up.
7. **History:** this is the first film recorded in the ledger. The previous Forlife films (dark, cinematic, 3D browser frames) are deliberately avoided: light wood, paper, hands, a top-down camera.

## Frames

### Frame 1 · Hook (0–2.5 s)
- key visual: a counter at night, seen straight down; a phone with an empty caption field, the cursor blinking
- moves first: sticky notes drop from off-frame, one per beat, their shadows tightening
- on-screen words: the notes only

### Frame 2 · Hook (2.5–6 s)
- key visual: the pile grows and buries the phone; the clock reads 23:58; a hand hovers, hesitating
- on-screen words: « Vous faites tout. » … « Seul. »
- beat hook: freeze + silence on the last half bar

### Frame 3 · Reveal (6–7.5 s)
- key visual: the hand sweeps the notes away; the Forlife coaster lands on the drop

### Frame 4 · The team (7.5–12.5 s)
- new component: six staff badges swing in on lanyards, all tied to the phone
- on-screen words: « Votre équipe marketing »

### Frame 5 · Proof moment (12.5–20 s)
- key visual: the phone fills the frame and the prompt types itself
- moves first: three cards fan out (text, visual, 9:16 video); a finger taps **Valider**
- after the tap: the cards slide into the network chips Facebook · Instagram · TikTok · LinkedIn · YouTube
- on-screen words: the prompt, « Valider »

### Frame 6 · Later (20–24 s)
- key visual: the clock ticks to Monday and the phone buzzes with notifications

### Frame 7 · CTA (24–30 s)
- key visual: the camera rises; a tidy counter; the phone at the centre with the six badges docked as icons
- on-screen words: « Toute une équipe marketing dans votre téléphone. » · Forlife Com · « Créez votre espace gratuitement » · the two WhatsApp numbers
