## Description

Called while still very young, in the thirteenth year of King Josiah, Jeremiah carried the word of God for some forty years. He dictated to Baruch a scroll that King Jehoiakim burned, wrote to the exiles in Babylon, and was cast into the dungeon during the siege. After the fall of Jerusalem he stayed among the people, then was taken to Egypt by those who had refused his message.

This book follows his life reign by reign, from Anathoth to Tahpanhes. Each chapter sheds light on the historical background, quotes the key verses and ends with lessons for today and questions for reflection, alone or in a group. A chronology and a map accompany the reading.

For the believer who reads the Bible, and for the servant of God who seeks to stay faithful when the word is unwelcome.
